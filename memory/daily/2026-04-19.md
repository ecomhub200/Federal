  session, to avoid over-architecting the graph on day one.

**Lessons Learned:**
- When seeding from a rich skill like `frontend-r2-connection`, the
  right move is a condensed concept article that *points* to the
  skill, not a duplicate. The skill is where the full authoritative
  R2 structure lives.
- Backlink discipline is worth maintaining as you go — fixing 17
  missing backlinks in one pass at the end of a batch (like we did at
  end of batch 2) works, but pre-seeding them while writing is cheap
  and keeps the graph coherent.
- A few articles (safety-focus predicates, warrants thresholds)
  document *best-current-understanding* from CLAUDE.md + docs, not
  verified code. Each flags that explicitly in its Sources section so
  a future session can tighten them against the real module.
